# Project: Bonsaify Mobile Storefront
Author: **Mohamad Tohari Maolana**

---

## Project Overview

A mobile storefront UI design for a premium botanical brand specializing in curated Bonsai trees. The design challenge was marrying serene "Zen" botanical aesthetics with high-converting e-commerce design patterns.

---

## Role & Objectives

* **Role:** Lead UI/UX Designer
* **Core Objectives:** Create an organic, high-end shopping experience featuring botanical photography, care guides, and a frictionless 2-step checkout flow.

---

## Design Stack & Tools

* **Interface & Layout:** Figma

---

## Final High-Fidelity Design Showcase

Built with rich earth-tone color palettes, editorial serif typography for botanical titles, and generous negative whitespace to convey luxury and calm.
