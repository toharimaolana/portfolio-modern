# MShopSensor — Blind Assist Smart Stick
Author: **Mohamad Tohari Maolana**

---

## Project Overview

**MShopSensor** (Tongkat Digital Sensor) is an advanced, sensor-guided smart stick designed to help visually impaired individuals navigate public spaces safely and independently by providing real-time obstacle alerts.

I developed a highly visual and professional product showcase landing page on Framer to highlight the smart stick features, sensor ranges, and device ergonomics to prospective users.

## My Role

**Framer Developer** — Engineered the responsive layout system, typography structures, and custom scroll-reveal animations in Framer to make the specs accessible and engaging.

## Key Features

- **Interactive Sensor Highlights:** Visual callouts explaining ultrasonic range, ground sensors, and vibration feedback.
- **Specification Board:** High-contrast tech sheets comparing battery life, warning speeds, and weight details.
- **Conversion Touchpoints:** Bold, high-contrast CTA blocks optimized for mobile tap inputs.

## Technical Stack

- **Platform:** Framer
- **Animations:** Scroll triggers & hover-state scaling
- **Live URL:** [mshopsensor.framer.website](https://mshopsensor.framer.website/)
